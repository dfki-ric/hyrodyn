Detailed Description

Exoskeletons to series-parallel hybrid mechanisms can be modeled in HyRoDyn using the concept of mimic joints in the URDF file.

A code snippet of mimic joint tag definition is shown

below:
<joint name=PASSIVE_JNAME type=JTYPE >
<parent link=PARENT >
<child link=CHILD >
<origin xyz=COORDS rpy=ANGLES >
<axis xyz=DIRECTION >
<mimic joint=DEPENDENT_JNAME multiplier=m offset=b >
</joint>
HyRoDyn will collect the multiplier and offset values from the URDF and transfers them to the loop closure function (LCF) of the exoskeleton.



where M is the multiplier matrix containing m (+1, -1, 0 as its entries) and B is the offset vector containing b (0s or angular offset, in rad) involved with the active joint frame.
The LCF of the exoskeletons differ in the sense that they can not be arranged in a block diagonal sense like LCF of the normal submechanism definition in HyRoDyn.
Assuming you named the 4 human joints as "right_human_shoulder_joint0", "right_human_shoulder_joint1", "right_human_shoulder_joint2", "right_human_elbow_joint" and 4 exoskeleton joints as "right_arm_shoulder_joint0", "right_arm_shoulder_joint1", "right_arm_shoulder_joint2", "right_arm_elbow_joint" here is what you will use in your submechanisms.yml file: exoskeletons:

  • name: "human_rightarm"
    around: "right_exo_arm"
    file_path: "RDAWRH.urdf"
    jointnames_dependent: ["right_arm_shoulder_joint0", "right_arm_shoulder_joint1", "right_arm_shoulder_joint2", "right_arm_elbow_joint"]
    jointnames_spanningtree: ["right_human_shoulder_joint0", "right_human_shoulder_joint1", "right_human_shoulder_joint2", "right_human_elbow_joint"]

For more examples, see data/hybrid/recupera/dual_arm_exo_with_human.
